# 06 Mandarin Speaking Coach

A Mandarin coach for overseas English-speaking learners, with an English interface and Mandarin practice. Introductions, dining and workplace lessons use fictional U.S. settings. All personal details may be fictional.

## Learning Flow

Say your level and topic. Beginners receive short Mandarin turns with English help; intermediate learners receive follow-up questions; advanced learners receive open-ended role-play. The coach offers at most one useful vocabulary or grammar correction per response, and honors repeat, explain, slow-down, topic and difficulty requests.

Request a summary before pressing End conversation. The coach is instructed to use actual learner utterances for feedback and to acknowledge insufficient evidence. There is no automatic course score or durable learning progress.

## Reading Support

Preview topic tabs show nine phrases with Chinese characters, tone-marked pinyin and English meanings. The header's Phrasebook opens the same material, including during a call. Browsing is independent of live coach state: name the desired topic aloud. Pinyin uses common spoken tone changes for 一 and 不. Other syllables show standard dictionary tones. The coach is instructed not to read romanized pinyin aloud.

## Limits

Recognized text cannot establish correct pronunciation, tones, accent or acoustic fluency. Standard tone explanations are teaching, not learner audio assessment. No HSK scoring, certification, real orders, meetings, enrollment, database or external tools are included. Transcript evidence remains in the current page until refresh.
